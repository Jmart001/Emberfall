# Emberfall — Questlines

*Six questlines, 34 quests. Existing quests (A Wanderer in Greenrest, Shadows Over Pineholt, Beneath the Ashen Barrow, the five side quests) are treated as canon and built upon — the first new main quest picks up the moment the existing Act I ends.*

**Difficulty tiers:** ★ Novice · ★★ Intermediate · ★★★ Experienced · ★★★★ Master · ★★★★★ Grandmaster

# Questline I — The Bound Ember *(Main Story, Acts II–IV)*

## I-1. The Cartographer's Complaint ★★
**Giver:** Steward Yara (Greenrest) · **Req:** Beneath the Ashen Barrow complete · **Length:** Short

Yara's missing-wagon problem finally gets investigated. The trail leads to the Weeping Stones, and to the first Ninefold sighting.

**Steps**
1. Speak with Steward Yara in Greenrest.
2. Examine the three abandoned wagons on the northern road (Pineholt border).
3. Search the wagons — find a hauling manifest signed with **nine dots in a circle**.
4. Show the manifest to Scholar Mira in Cinderforge.
5. Investigate the Weeping Stones in Millrow Farms at night.
6. Defeat 2 **Ninefold acolytes**. Return to Yara.

**Dialogue sample**
> **Yara:** I have counted every wagon that never arrived, and I have stopped believing it is bandits.
> **Yara:** Bandits take the cargo and leave the ash churned up. These leave the ash *smooth*, Wanderer. Swept. Who sweeps a road they've just robbed?
> **You:** Someone who doesn't want to be tracked.
> **Yara:** Someone *tidy*. Which frightens me considerably more.

> *(At the Weeping Stones)*
> **Ninefold acolyte:** You shouldn't be here. The stones are warm tonight — that means they're listening.
> **Ninefold acolyte:** There are eight more asleep, ember-touched. Eight! And your Wardens spent four centuries learning to say *no* to one of them.

**Reward:** 400 coins · 1,200 Attack XP · Journal entry *"The Nine Dots"* · unlocks Ninefold acolyte spawns across all regions.

## I-2. The Cracked Verse ★★★
**Giver:** Scholar Mira (Cinderforge) · **Req:** I-1 · **Length:** Medium

Mira works out that the Oath seal in Thornwood is failing, and sends you to hear it for yourself. **Gateway quest into Questline II.**

**Steps**
1. Speak with Mira. She gives you the **Warden's ledger** (quest item).
2. Travel to Thornwood; ask Elder Willow to perform the Verse.
3. Listen to the full Verse at the Singing Grove — a **puzzle:** the Choir sings 8 lines; 3 are wrong. Identify all three from the ledger's fragments.
4. Report the corrupted lines to Mira.
5. Defeat the **Gallows Echo** (level 24) at the Gallows Oak — an oath-breaker's shade that manifests when the Verse is sung wrong.
6. Return to Mira.

**Puzzle detail — The Verse**
The ledger gives fragments of the true Oath. The Choir sings a corrupted version. The player must pick the wrong lines from a list of eight. The three corruptions each swapped a *binding* word for a *comforting* one — the tell is that the true Verse is cruel and the corrupted one is kind:

| Choir sings | True line |
|---|---|
| "We keep the fire **warm**" | "We keep the fire **shut**" |
| "Nine names to **hold** the door" | "Nine names to **bar** the door" |
| "Until the fire **rests**" | "Until the fire **forgets**" |

> **Elder Willow:** Four hundred years we've sung it at every burial and every naming. You're telling me it's a *lock*?
> **You:** I'm telling you it's a lock, and someone's been filing the teeth off it.
> **Elder Willow:** ...My mother taught me that song. Her mother taught her. Which of them softened it, do you think? And did they know?

**Reward:** 500 coins · 1,500 Prayer XP · **Verse-keeper's tabard** (def 5) · unlocks Questline II.

## I-3. Ash and Silence ★★★
**Giver:** Scout Vale (Ashfall Wastes) · **Req:** I-2 · **Length:** Medium

Vale hasn't heard the eastern bell in six days. The Warden's charge says the Ashen Warden wakes if *the seal breaks and the eastern bell falls silent*. One of those has already happened.

**Steps**
1. Speak with Scout Vale at the Barrow door.
2. Find the eastern signal tower along Rowan's Line (Pineholt) — the brazier is cold and the watchman is gone.
3. Search the tower: find a bedroll, thirty years of tally marks, and a Warden-Commander's seal.
4. Bring the seal to Mira. **Reveal:** it belongs to Alder Ashvale, presumed dead thirty years.
5. Travel to Alder's Camp in the Wastes. Find it occupied, tidy, and warm.
6. **Confrontation (no combat):** Alder talks to you. He is calm, kind, and completely certain.
7. Return to Mira with what he told you.

**Dialogue sample — first meeting with Alder**
> **Alder Ashvale:** Sit. You've walked a long way and I've been waiting thirty years; we can both spare a minute.
> **Alder:** They told you it's a monster down there. Did they tell you it *cries*? Not roars. Cries. Six hours a night, every night, for four hundred years, and I have sat here and listened to every one of them.
> **You:** It burned the world.
> **Alder:** It reached for us. That's not the same thing, and you of all people should know the difference — you've reached for something and hurt it, haven't you? Everyone has. We just don't usually do it at the scale of kingdoms.
> **Alder:** Go on. Go back to Mira. Tell her the old man's gone soft in the ash. But when you're standing at that door — and you will be — remember that somebody has been down there alone since before your great-grandmother drew breath.

**Reward:** 600 coins · 2,000 Defence XP · Journal entry *"The Commander's Argument"* · Alder becomes a recurring NPC.

## I-4. The Guttering Light ★★★★
**Giver:** Brother Edric (Frostmere) · **Req:** I-3 · **Length:** Long · **Gateway to Questline III**

The Watch seal is dying under the glacier. Fifty-four of 108 wicks are out. Relighting them is not the problem — *keeping them lit* is.

**Steps**
1. Speak with Brother Edric at the Frostmere wayfire.
2. Descend the Glass Shelf crevasse into the Wayfire Vault.
3. **Puzzle — The Hundred and Eight:** relight wicks in the correct order. Lighting a wick out of sequence snuffs its two neighbours. The order is encoded in the Verse from I-2 (nine names, twelve wicks each).
4. Survive the **Rime Custodian** (level 33), which wakes when the 54th wick lights.
5. Discover the vault's second door — sealed from the *inside*.
6. Report to Edric. He admits the order stopped maintaining the vault eighty years ago because the last man who went down didn't come back up.

**Reward:** 900 coins · 3,000 Firemaking XP · 2,000 Prayer XP · **Wayfire lantern** (light source + 3 def) · unlocks Questline III & the Wayfire Vault raid.

## I-5. What the Bellows Made ★★★★
**Giver:** Torren (Cinderforge) · **Req:** I-4 · **Length:** Long · **Gateway to Questline IV**

The Deep Bellows have forged something with the Ember's own heat, and Torren wants it stopped before the Quenchers burn the town down over it.

**Steps**
1. Speak with Torren at the Cinderforge anvil.
2. Gather evidence: 3 **living ingots** (metal that stays hot) from Slagfields cinder fiends.
3. Descend the Throat. Speak with the three Deep Bellows masters — **Vurn**, **Hesk**, and **the Third**, who does not speak.
4. **Choice:** side with the Tenders (let the forging continue) or the Quenchers (sabotage the Great Bellows).
5. Either path: fight the **Bellows-Wrought** (level 38), the thing they made — a suit of armour with nothing inside it and a heartbeat.
6. Return to Torren.

**Branch consequences**
- **Tender path:** Emberwright shops sell emberforged gear at 20% off; Cinderforge volcano activity increases; Quencher NPCs turn hostile.
- **Quencher path:** volcano calms; ember ingot drop rates fall 25%; you gain the **Quencher's oath** (immune to burn damage in raids).

> **Vurn:** We didn't *make* it. We asked, and the fire answered, and this is what came up the shaft.
> **Hesk:** It has a heartbeat, Wanderer. Nothing's inside it and it has a heartbeat. Vurn thinks that's a triumph.
> **Vurn:** It *is* a triumph.
> **Hesk:** It's a *symptom*.

**Reward:** 1,200 coins · 3,500 Smithing XP · **Emberwright's hammer** (weapon, spec: *Reforge* — repairs 20% of your max HP as armour) · unlocks Questline IV & the Deep Bellows raid.

## I-6. The Drowned Petition ★★★★
**Giver:** Healer Sable (Sablemarsh) · **Req:** I-5 · **Length:** Long · **Gateway to Questline V**

Marsh-fever is Ember-heat leaking up through the water. The only people who know the marsh's deep channels are the Quiet Court, and they have been dead and polite for four hundred years.

**Steps**
1. Speak with Healer Sable in Mirehaven.
2. Wait for low water; enter the Drowned Court.
3. **Puzzle — Procedure:** the Court will not speak to a petitioner without correct standing. Acquire, in order: a *sponsor* (any Sablemarsh NPC), a *seal of introduction* (Fenwick), *appropriate dress* (Court mourning silks, crafted), and a *petition in triplicate* (three identical written copies — the third must be written *underwater*).
4. Petition the **Lord Chamberlain Vess** for the deep-channel maps.
5. He agrees — for a *fee in memory*. You must permanently give up one Journal lore entry of your choosing.
6. Deliver the maps to Sable.

> **Lord Chamberlain Vess:** Your petition is in order. I confess I did not expect that. The last petitioner brought only urgency, and urgency is not a document.
> **You:** People are dying up there.
> **Vess:** People were dying up there when I was alive, Wanderer. That is what people do; it is very nearly their defining feature. Procedure is what remains.

**Reward:** 1,400 coins · 4,000 Herblore XP · **Court silks** (def 7, +poison immunity) · unlocks Questline V & the Drowned Court raid.

## I-7. The Door and the Doorknob ★★★★★
**Giver:** Scholar Mira · **Req:** I-6 + all three seal questlines (II, III, IV) complete · **Length:** Very long

Kessa steals the Ember relic. Alder makes his move. Everything converges at the Barrow door.

**Steps**
1. Mira's study is ransacked; the Ember relic is gone. Track Kessa.
2. Follow her through three regions (Thornwood → Frostmere → Ashfall) — each leg has a **pursuit encounter** rather than a fight: she sets obstacles, you clear them under a timer.
3. Corner her at the Barrow door. **She is not the villain and says so.**
4. **Boss fight — Kessa, Warden-Apprentice (level 45).** She fights to *lose slowly*, buying Alder time. At 25% HP she stands down.
5. Alder opens the first seal. The eastern bell rings once and cracks.
6. **Boss fight — Warden-Commander Alder Ashvale (level 52).** Three phases: Oath (he quotes the true Verse and it *binds you* — you must break free by moving), Watch (he splits into four lantern-shades, only one real), Cinders (he burns his own life to hit harder as he dies).
7. Alder falls. With his last breath he tells you the third option exists.

**Dialogue sample — Kessa cornered**
> **Kessa:** Go on then. You've been wanting to since Cinderforge.
> **You:** You took the relic.
> **Kessa:** I took the *doorknob*, and I gave it to a man who has listened to that thing cry every night for thirty years. You've listened to it for what — an afternoon? And you're certain, aren't you. Gods, you're so certain.
> **Kessa:** I'm not going to win this fight. I know that. I just have to make it *long*.

**Reward:** 3,000 coins · 8,000 Attack XP · **Ashvale signet** (unlocks Questline VI) · Journal entry *"The Third Option"*.

## I-8. The Tending ★★★★★
**Giver:** *(self-initiated at the Barrow door)* · **Req:** I-7 + Questline VI + all 7 regional raids cleared · **Length:** Finale

The three-way choice. Each ending is fully playable and permanently changes the world state.

**Steps**
1. Descend to the Barrow's tenth chamber — which did not exist before now.
2. Speak with the **Ashen Warden** (Cass Ashvale). She can finally talk, because you carry the signet of her own house.
3. **Speak with the Ember.** It has never been spoken to. It does not know words; it answers in heat, and the conversation is a rhythm puzzle — you match its pulse to reply.
4. **Choose:** RE-SEAL, RELEASE, or TEND.

**The Ashen Warden speaks**
> **Ashen Warden:** Four hundred and six years, and you are the first to say my name. Cass. I had begun to think I had made it up.
> **Ashen Warden:** I will tell you what nobody told me, because I would have liked to be told. It is not a monster. It is not a god. It is a *fire that was left on*, and everyone who could have turned it off is dead, and it has been reaching for a hand to hold since before your language existed.
> **Ashen Warden:** I have held the door for four centuries. I never once opened it to look in. That is my great cowardice and I have had a very long time to consider it.

**Endings**

| Ending | Requirement | World state after |
|---|---|---|
| **RE-SEAL** | Repair all 3 seals | Ashfall recedes; marsh-fever cures; volcano sleeps. Regional NPCs thank you. A single grey chat line every 30 minutes of play: *"Somewhere far below, something is still crying."* |
| **RELEASE** | Carry the relic to the door | Ember rises. All 7 regions gain a permanent hostile "ember-touched" spawn tier and new endgame drops. Half the named NPCs die; the survivors will not speak to you. |
| **TEND** | Complete everything, then take the Cinders seal | You become the next Ashen Warden. The save converts to **Warden mode**: your character is now an NPC at the Barrow, and you begin again as a *new* Wanderer — who can visit and talk to your own previous character. |

**Reward:** all endings grant **Ashen crown** (best-in-slot helm) · 15,000 XP spread across all combat skills · title *"the Last Warden"*.

# Questline II — The Choir of Thorns *(Thornwood / Oath seal)*

## II-1. The Song That Locks ★★
Elder Willow tests whether the Choir can even *sing* the true verses. Collect 4 **verse-shards** from Bramble Deep boars that have been "herded" — the herder left the shards behind.
**Reward:** 300 coins, 800 Slayer XP, **Thorn-singer's charm**.

## II-2. What Herds the Boars ★★★
Something is arranging Thornwood's wildlife into shapes. Track it through the Bramble Deep — a **maze that rearranges** every 30 seconds; you must map it by leaving markers. **Boss:** the **Bramblewright** (level 27), a thing of thorns and antlers that fights by moving the walls.
**Reward:** 600 coins, 2,000 Hunter XP, **Bramble bow** (ranged 9, spec: *Root* — snares the target 5s).

## II-3. The Gallows Chorus ★★★
Restore the hanged oath-breakers' verses to let the Bell ring true. Ritual at the Gallows Oak: seven shades sing seven lines and you must respond with the *eighth* each time, in the correct register (a **call-and-response rhythm puzzle**, difficulty scales each round).
**Reward:** 900 coins, 2,500 Prayer XP, **Gallows lantern**.

## II-4. Recasting the Bell ★★★★
The Verse Bell must be melted and recast with the true Oath sung into the mould — which means the Choir must sing continuously for the full pour while **the Ember's heat tries to crack it**. Escort/defence encounter: protect 5 singers from waves for 6 minutes; every singer lost weakens the final seal.
**Boss:** **Discord** (level 36) — a sound, not a body. Damages you when you *stand still in the wrong note zone*; safe tiles shift with the music.
**Reward:** 1,800 coins, 5,000 Prayer XP, **Oath seal restored** (main story gate), **Bellringer's mantle** (def 9).

# Questline III — The Long Watch *(Frostmere / Watch seal)*

## III-1. The Man Who Didn't Come Up ★★★
Find the remains of the last vault-keeper. He is not dead. He is in **Rimehollow**, frozen mid-stride with everyone else, and still aware.
**Puzzle:** thaw exactly one person without thawing the village (heat management — the wayfire lantern warms a 1-tile radius; you must chain warm tiles without overlapping).
**Reward:** 700 coins, 2,200 Firemaking XP, **Rimehollow key**.

## III-2. One Hundred and Eight ★★★
Re-wick the vault permanently. Gather 108 **coldwicks** from Glass Shelf crevasses while frost trolls hunt you — a **stealth-adjacent** quest; trolls track heat, so you must periodically extinguish your own lantern and navigate dark.
**Reward:** 1,000 coins, 3,000 Crafting XP, **Coldwick bundle**.

## III-3. The Door Sealed From Inside ★★★★
Open the Wayfire Vault's inner door. Whatever sealed it is still behind it — and it sealed itself in *voluntarily*, four hundred years ago, because it knew what it was becoming.
**Boss:** **Warden Halvard, the Willing Cold** (level 40). Fights defensively and *apologises* between attacks. Mechanic: he freezes the floor progressively; the arena shrinks each phase.
**Reward:** 1,600 coins, 4,500 Defence XP, **Halvard's vow** (shield, def 12, spec: *Endure* — negate the next hit entirely).

## III-4. Keeping It Lit ★★★★
Rebuild the Watch. Recruit 5 keepers from across the regions (each is a small persuasion sub-quest with a *different* requirement — one wants coin, one wants their brother avenged, one wants proof the Wardens aren't lying, one wants a hot meal, one just needs asking twice).
**Reward:** 2,000 coins, 6,000 Prayer XP, **Watch seal restored**, **Keeper's brand** (permanent +5% run energy regen).

# Questline IV — The Deep Bellows *(Cinderforge / Emberwright schism)*

## IV-1. Living Metal ★★★
Investigate ingots that never cool. Requires forging a blade that can *cut* them — a Smithing puzzle where you must quench in the wrong medium on purpose (marsh water, not oil).
**Reward:** 800 coins, 2,500 Smithing XP, **Everhot chisel**.

## IV-2. The Third Master ★★★
The Third Bellows master has not spoken in eleven years. Find out why. **Answer:** she has been holding a note continuously — a single sustained sound that is the only thing keeping the Throat's pressure stable. If she stops to speak, the volcano vents.
**Puzzle:** take over the note. A sustained-input minigame while you interview her by *written questions*.
**Reward:** 1,100 coins, 3,000 Mining XP, **Throat-warden's mask**.

## IV-3. The Great Bellows ★★★★
Depending on your I-5 branch, either fire the Great Bellows to full or sabotage it. Both versions are timed multi-stage machine operations with sabotage/defence waves.
**Boss:** **Slagborn** (level 42) — a Colossus-class enemy that *absorbs* your damage type and adapts; you must rotate between melee, ranged, and magic every 20 seconds or it becomes immune.
**Reward:** 2,200 coins, 6,000 Smithing XP, **Emberforged crown** (helm, def 10).

## IV-4. What the Fire Wants ★★★★
The Deep Bellows have been *taking dictation*. The Ember has been giving them designs. Recover the design ledger and learn the Ember is trying to build **hands** — a body to hold something with.
**Reward:** 2,500 coins, 7,000 Smithing XP, Journal entry *"The Hand Designs"*, unlocks **emberforged tier 2** smithing recipes.

# Questline V — The Quiet Court *(Sablemarsh)*

## V-1. Appropriate Dress ★★
Craft Court mourning silks. Requires 6 cave silk, 3 bog moss dye, and a pattern held by the Court itself — a catch-22 solved by finding a drowned tailor's corpse.
**Reward:** 400 coins, 1,500 Crafting XP.

## V-2. In Triplicate ★★★
Write a petition three times; the third underwater. **Puzzle:** an air-management sequence — you have limited breath, ink runs, and errors force a restart. Marsh eel (cures poison) is effectively required.
**Reward:** 700 coins, 2,000 Herblore XP, **Waterproof case**.

## V-3. The Fever's Source ★★★★
Follow the deep channels to Fevermire, where the water is warm. Find the Ember's heat rising through a fissure — and a **Court census-taker** counting the dead, who has been counting for four hundred years and is only at 1,431 of 12,000.
**Boss:** **The Tally** (level 39) — it fights by *counting your actions*; repeat any attack type three times and it becomes immune to it permanently for the fight.
**Reward:** 1,700 coins, 5,000 Herblore XP, **Fevermire cure** (mass-cures Sablemarsh, changes NPC dialogue region-wide).

## V-4. Adjournment ★★★★
The Quiet Court must be dissolved so its members can finally die. Requires satisfying every outstanding item on a four-hundred-year-old agenda — 9 tasks across all regions, each absurd and each genuinely someone's last wish.
**Reward:** 2,400 coins, 6,500 Prayer XP, **Chamberlain's seal** (def 8, grants NPC discount 15% everywhere).

# Questline VI — Blood of Ashvale *(Personal)*

## VI-1. The Signet ★★★★
The Ashvale signet from I-7 opens a door in the Thornwood hills that was never there. Ashvale Keep is empty, clean, and something is still keeping house.
**Reward:** 1,000 coins, unlocks Ashvale Keep region.

## VI-2. The Long Gallery ★★★★
Portraits of every Ashvale. The last one is you — painted four hundred years ago. **Puzzle:** restore 6 damaged portraits in the correct generational order using clues from the Warden Archive.
**Reward:** 1,500 coins, 4,000 Prayer XP, Journal entries revealing the bloodline.

## VI-3. What Keeps House ★★★★
Meet the thing that has cleaned Ashvale Keep for four centuries: **the Steward**, a bound servant who does not know its masters are dead and will not be told. It cannot be killed while it believes it has duties. You must *dismiss it properly* — which requires finding written authority from a living Ashvale. You are one. You must write your own household dismissal.
**Reward:** 1,800 coins, 5,000 Crafting XP, **Ashvale housekeys**.

## VI-4. The Eighth Grave ★★★★★
Seven Cinders Wardens have graves in the Family Crypt. There is an eighth, marked with *your* name and a date four hundred years ago.
**Reveal:** you are not a descendant. You are **the eighth Cinders Warden** — you gave your life to the seal alongside the other seven, and the Ember, not understanding death, has been slowly *putting you back together* for four hundred years because it wanted its tender returned. Your memory loss is not damage. It is **incompleteness.**
**Boss:** **The Unfinished** (level 48) — the parts of you the Ember got wrong. It uses your own equipped gear and copies your combat style each phase.
**Reward:** 3,000 coins, 8,000 XP across all combat skills, **Cinders fragment**.

## VI-5. The Hollow Throne ★★★★★
Sit in the Ashvale throne and receive four hundred years of your own memories at once. Survive it — a pure **endurance encounter** against your own recovered past, with no gear and no items, only what you remember how to do.
**Reward:** full memory restoration, **Ashvale plate** (best-in-slot body), unlocks the **TEND** ending in I-8.

# Quest Design Principles

1. **Every quest teaches or tests a mechanic** before the raid that needs it. I-4's wick puzzle teaches sequencing → the Wayfire Vault raid is built on sequencing.
2. **Bosses scale with tier.** ★★ bosses have one mechanic. ★★★★★ bosses have three phases with distinct mechanics each.
3. **No quest is a pure fetch.** Every collection step has a complication (the item is guarded, or underwater, or the giver is lying about what it's for).
4. **Antagonists get the best argument.** Alder, Kessa, Vess, and the Ember are all *right about something*. The player should be uncomfortable at least twice per questline.
5. **Rewards are horizontal where possible.** Not just bigger numbers — light sources, immunities, discounts, XP modifiers, and access.
