# Emberfall - Revised Future State

## Vision

Emberfall is a single-player 3D fantasy RPG inspired by the deliberate rhythm,
readable interactions, and long-term progression of classic point-and-click
adventures and Old School RuneScape.

The player is the Wanderer, an ember-touched figure found without memories at
the edge of Greenrest Vale. They explore a wounded but inhabited world, learn
ordinary trades, uncover the history of the Wardens of Ash, and discover that
they are not descended from the lost Wardens. They are the eighth Cinders
Warden, slowly reconstructed by the lonely Hearth-Spirit buried beneath the
world.

Emberfall is not a branching narrative. Every major quest has one canonical
outcome, and the complete campaign leads to one ending: the Wanderer chooses to
TEND the Ember.

## Player Experience

The finished game should feel:

- Deliberate rather than frantic
- Mysterious without relying on long exposition
- Lonely in the wild and warm in settlements
- Easy to read despite being fully 3D
- Rewarding for preparation, positioning, and mastery
- Rich in optional activity without losing the main story

The camera uses an elevated third-person perspective. The player clicks the
world to move and clicks characters, enemies, resources, and objects to
interact. Movement is smooth, but combat and gathering retain Emberfall's
600-millisecond action tick.

## Design Pillars

### A Living 3D World

Regions are compact, handcrafted spaces rather than a huge seamless map. Roads,
landmarks, lighting, silhouettes, and elevation make navigation understandable
without constant reliance on the minimap.

Settlements contain useful services, recurring characters, small stories, and
visible signs of the region's problems. The wilderness creates contrast through
danger, distance, weather, and limited safety.

### Deliberate Progression

Skills support exploration and combat. They are not separate minigames added
only to increase the feature count. Each retained skill must unlock useful
equipment, routes, supplies, quest solutions, or raid advantages.

### One Canonical Journey

Quests may allow different dialogue tones or different methods of completing an
objective, but they never create mutually exclusive world states. Choices
reveal character; they do not split the campaign.

Every quest has:

- One starting state
- A short sequence of explicit objectives
- One completion state
- One canonical reward and world change
- A mechanical or narrative purpose

### Memorable Raids

Emberfall contains four major raids. Each marks the end of a story act, tests
mechanics taught in surrounding quests, and permanently advances the campaign.
All raids are designed for one player first.

## Scope

### Regions

The final campaign uses seven primary regions:

1. **Greenrest Vale** - farms, castle, docks, and the safest surviving land
2. **Pineholt** - northern road, watchtowers, and goblin pressure
3. **Thornwood** - the Choir of Thorns and the failing Oath seal
4. **Frostmere** - the guttering Watch seal beneath the glacier
5. **Cinderforge** - Emberwright industry and the cost of using Ember heat
6. **Sablemarsh** - marsh-fever, drowned history, and the Quiet Court
7. **Ashfall Wastes** - the Barrow, Alder's camp, and the wound above the Ember

Ashvale Keep is a late-game dungeon within the Thornwood frontier, not a full
open-world region. The Ash Sea, Emberreach, and Ninefold Deep are reserved for a
future expansion and are not part of the initial game.

### Skills

The initial 3D game focuses on:

- Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged, Magic, Hitpoints, and Prayer
- Fishing and Cooking
- Mining and Smithing
- Woodcutting, Firemaking, and Crafting
- Slayer as the primary repeatable combat activity

Farming, Hunter, Herblore, Thieving, and Fletching remain possible later
additions. They should not block completion of the 3D foundation.

### Multiplayer

The initial game is single-player. No raid, quest, puzzle, or progression gate
requires another player. Cooperative play may be evaluated after the complete
single-player campaign works.

## Canonical Story

### The Truth of the Ember

The Ember is one of nine Hearth-Spirits placed beneath the world to warm it.
The ninth woke and reached upward for someone to tend it. It burned everything
it touched because it could not understand the difference between warmth and
destruction.

The Wardens buried it behind three seals:

- **The Oath**, held in the Verse Bell in Thornwood
- **The Watch**, held in the Wayfire beneath Frostmere
- **The Cinders**, held by Cass Ashvale, the Ashen Warden

The relic recovered from the Ashen Barrow is not a seal. It is the mechanism
that allows a Warden to open or close the final door.

### The Wanderer

The Wanderer is the eighth Cinders Warden. They died during the Binding and
were never fully laid to rest. Over four centuries, the Ember reconstructed
them and called them back.

Their amnesia is not an injury. At the beginning of the game, they are literally
incomplete. Their memories return as the seals are encountered and repaired.

### Alder

Warden-Commander Alder Ashvale has listened to the Ember for thirty years. He
correctly understands that it is alive and suffering, but believes releasing it
is the only compassionate answer. He serves as the main antagonist because his
solution would destroy the people who now live above it.

Alder is right about the Ember and wrong about what must be done.

### The Ending

The player does not choose between re-sealing, releasing, and tending. Those
possibilities are represented by Mira, Alder, and Cass throughout the story.

The Wanderer ultimately rejects both abandonment and destruction. They take the
Cinders seal from Cass, descend to the Ember, and become its tender. The Ember
stops reaching toward the surface because someone is finally present with it.

The land begins to heal. The Wanderer becomes the new Ashen Warden and remains
beneath the Barrow. This is the only ending.

## Campaign Structure

The campaign contains sixteen main quests across four acts. Existing side
activities and small regional stories can remain optional, but they do not
branch the central narrative.

### Act I - The Relic

#### 1. A Wanderer in Greenrest

Learn movement, shopping, fishing, cooking, and combat. Elowen reveals that the
old Warden tablets began glowing when the Wanderer appeared.

**Outcome:** Rowan asks the Wanderer to investigate the northern road.

#### 2. Shadows Over Pineholt

Help Captain Rowan contain displaced goblin raiders and discover that something
is driving them south.

**Outcome:** Rowan sends the Wanderer to Scholar Mira.

#### 3. Beneath the Ashen Barrow

Recover the seal-key, enter the Barrow, confront the Ashen Warden, and return
with the Ember relic.

**Outcome:** The relic recognizes the Wanderer. Mira begins investigating their
connection to the Wardens.

#### 4. The Ashwright's Gambit

Investigate stolen Ember materials, find Kessa's camp, and stop an unstable
extraction.

**Outcome:** Kessa survives and reveals that Alder still lives in the Wastes.

### Act II - The Oath

#### 5. The Song That Locks

Travel to Thornwood and recover the missing portions of the Warden verse.

**Outcome:** The Choir learns that its oldest song maintains the Oath seal.

#### 6. The Gallows Verse

Explore the Bramble Deep and Gallows Oak to learn why the verse was deliberately
forgotten.

**Outcome:** Elder Willow agrees to restore the complete Oath despite its cost.

#### 7. The Bell That Remembers

Repair the Verse Bell and defend the Choir while it rehearses the true Oath.

**Outcome:** The Verse Bell raid opens.

#### 8. Raid: The Verse Bell

Protect the Choir and fight Discord while harmonic and dissonant zones move
through the arena.

**Outcome:** The Oath seal is restored, and the Wanderer recovers their first
complete memory of the Binding.

### Act III - The Watch

#### 9. The Guttering Light

Reach Frostmere and investigate why the Wayfire is failing.

**Outcome:** The submerged Wayfire Vault is located.

#### 10. One Hundred and Eight

Recover coldwicks, learn the correct lighting sequence, and reopen the Vault.

**Outcome:** The Watch can be restored, but its keeper must be released.

#### 11. The Commander's Argument

Find Alder in the Ashfall Wastes and hear the truth he learned from the Ember.

**Outcome:** The Wanderer understands that the Ember is a suffering
Hearth-Spirit, not a malicious god.

#### 12. Raid: The Wayfire Vault

Manage a finite Flame resource for sight, warmth, and damage. Release the Rime
Custodian and rekindle the Watch.

**Outcome:** The Watch seal is restored, and the Wanderer remembers dying in
the Binding.

### Act IV - The Cinders

#### 13. What the Bellows Made

Investigate living metal in Cinderforge and stop an Ember-forged construct.

**Outcome:** Torren helps create equipment capable of surviving the final
descent. The Emberwright conflict resolves around controlled use of Ember heat.

#### 14. The Drowned Petition

Enter the Quiet Court, obtain the deep-channel records, and cure the source of
marsh-fever.

**Outcome:** The records reveal a hidden route to Ashvale Keep and confirm the
Wanderer's identity.

#### 15. The Hollow Throne

Enter Ashvale Keep, recover the Cinders history, and confront a reflection built
from the Wanderer's own equipment and combat style.

**Outcome:** The Wanderer's memories return completely. Alder takes the relic
and opens the path to the final Barrow chamber.

#### 16. Raid: The Tending

Pursue Alder through a transformed Ashen Barrow. Defeat him without denying the
truth of his argument, release Cass from the Cinders seal, and speak to the
Ember through heat and rhythm.

**Outcome:** The Wanderer becomes the Ember's tender and the new Ashen Warden.

## Raid Roster

### Raid 1 - The Ashen Barrow

**Act:** I  
**Purpose:** Introduce raid structure and the Cinders mystery  
**Core mechanic:** Random chamber order, darkness, doors, and burning sigils  
**Final encounter:** The Ashen Warden  
**Permanent result:** The player recovers the Ember relic

The current Barrow is the mechanical starting point. Its 3D version should use
compact rooms, strong landmarks, readable door states, and telegraphed floor
hazards.

### Raid 2 - The Verse Bell

**Act:** II  
**Purpose:** Restore the Oath seal  
**Core mechanic:** Moving harmonic and dissonant zones  
**Final encounter:** Discord  
**Permanent result:** The true Oath is sung and the seal is restored

Sound improves anticipation, but every rhythm and danger must also have a clear
visual indicator. Muted play remains fully supported.

### Raid 3 - The Wayfire Vault

**Act:** III  
**Purpose:** Restore the Watch seal  
**Core mechanic:** A finite Flame resource spent on sight, warmth, or power  
**Final encounter:** The Rime Custodian  
**Permanent result:** The Custodian is released and the Watch is rekindled

The raid is tuned for one player. Flame decisions happen through quick,
readable controls and never require simultaneous party actions.

### Raid 4 - The Tending

**Act:** IV  
**Purpose:** Resolve Alder, Cass, the Wanderer, and the Ember  
**Core mechanic:** Mechanics from the previous seals return in combined form  
**Final encounters:** Alder, then the Ember conversation  
**Permanent result:** The Wanderer becomes the new Ashen Warden

The Hollow Throne mirror encounter is the final quest boss immediately before
this raid, not a separate repeatable raid.

## Quest Outcome Rule

All existing and future quests follow this rule:

> A player may choose how the Wanderer speaks or how an objective is approached,
> but completing the quest always produces the same reward, unlock, and world
> state.

Examples:

- The Broken Road always ends with the refugee turned over to Mara, who places
  them in service protecting the road rather than imprisoning or releasing them.
- Kessa may be challenged or heard out, but she always fails to take the relic
  and later leads the Wanderer to Alder.
- The Emberwright dispute always ends with controlled use of the Bellows. The
  player helps prove that neither uncontrolled feeding nor total quenching is
  sustainable.
- The Quiet Court is always released after providing its records.
- The final story always ends with TEND.

## 3D Technical Direction

### Platform

The first 3D implementation remains a browser game. Three.js renders the world,
characters, lighting, particles, and camera while existing JavaScript formulas
are retained where practical.

### Architecture

Gameplay rules must not directly draw or manipulate scene objects. The 3D
conversion should introduce clear boundaries:

- **GameState** - serializable character and world state
- **SaveService** - versioned saving, loading, and migration
- **QuestSystem** - objective progression and canonical outcomes
- **CombatSystem** - tick timing, accuracy, damage, death, and rewards
- **InventorySystem** - items, equipment, banking, and crafting inputs
- **WorldService** - zones, entities, portals, resources, and triggers
- **NavigationController** - click-to-move and interaction range
- **ThreeRenderer** - camera, models, animation, effects, and selection
- **UIController** - journal, inventory, shops, dialogue, and accessibility

### World Construction

The current tile map is reference material, not the final 3D level format.
Each region becomes a separate authored zone containing:

- Terrain and elevation
- Walkable navigation surfaces
- Static collision
- Entity and resource spawn points
- Interaction anchors
- Quest triggers
- Region transitions
- Lighting and weather settings

The zones may still align to an invisible logical grid where that improves
combat readability, but visual geometry is not limited to square tiles.

### Art Direction

Use stylized low-poly models with painted textures, strong silhouettes, warm
settlement lighting, and restrained environmental detail. The game should look
intentional at a moderate asset budget and remain readable from the elevated
camera.

## Development Roadmap

### Milestone 1 - Design Lock

- Adopt this document as the authoritative future state
- Convert existing quest branches to canonical outcomes
- Mark unused regions, raids, NPCs, and items as expansion concepts
- Produce a small content manifest for the Greenrest slice

### Milestone 2 - 3D Greenrest Vertical Slice

Build a complete 3D version of **A Wanderer in Greenrest** with:

- Elevated camera and click-to-move navigation
- Greenrest plaza, shop, range, docks, and nearby combat field
- The Wanderer, Elowen, Murphy, and one trader
- Fishing, cooking, inventory, equipment, and goblin combat
- Dialogue, quest tracking, saving, loading, and settings
- Desktop performance and basic responsive UI validation

The milestone is complete only when a new player can begin, finish the quest,
save, reload, and continue without developer controls.

### Milestone 3 - Combat and Content Pipeline

- Finalize melee, ranged, magic, prayer, enemies, loot, and death
- Establish the GLTF model and animation pipeline
- Establish zone, NPC, monster, item, and quest data formats
- Create reusable interaction, hazard, boss, and dialogue components

### Milestone 4 - Act I and Ashen Barrow

- Build Pineholt and the Ashfall approach
- Complete the first four quests
- Convert the Ashen Barrow into the first 3D raid
- Validate progression from a new save through the end of Act I

### Milestone 5 - Acts II and III

- Build Thornwood and Frostmere
- Complete the Oath and Watch quest arcs
- Ship the Verse Bell and Wayfire Vault raids

### Milestone 6 - Final Act

- Build Cinderforge, Sablemarsh, Ashvale Keep, and the final Barrow state
- Complete the last four quests
- Ship the Hollow Throne encounter and The Tending raid
- Implement the canonical ending and postgame state

### Milestone 7 - Polish

- Balance progression and rewards
- Improve accessibility, controls, onboarding, and performance
- Complete audio, music, cinematics, and visual effects
- Run full save migration and campaign completion testing

## Definition of Done

The initial game is complete when:

- The full sixteen-quest campaign is playable in 3D
- All four raids are complete solo
- Every quest has one canonical outcome
- The TEND ending and postgame world state work
- The game can be completed from a fresh save without admin tools
- Saving and migration survive updates
- Combat and navigation remain readable on supported screen sizes
- Expansion concepts are not required to understand or finish the story
